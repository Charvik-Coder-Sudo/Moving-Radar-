Optional aircraft model for the 3D view.

Place ONE of these files here to replace the built-in procedural jet:
    aircraft.stl   aircraft.obj   aircraft.ply

Requirements (the model is posed with the body-FRD attitude matrix):
    units      metres, origin at the centre of gravity
    +X         nose (forward)
    +Y         right wing
    +Z         DOWN (so the fin points along -Z)

The model is drawn enlarged by display.aircraft_scale.
